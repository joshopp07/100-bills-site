The Medicare Covers Teeth, Eyes, and Ears Act

The Medicare Covers Teeth, Eyes, and Ears Act widens what Medicare pays for. It covers dental, eye, and hearing care, from checkups and fillings to glasses and hearing aids. Any person can choose hospice without giving up treatment, the way children on Medicaid can today, and a patient kept under observation in a hospital pays no more than an admitted patient. Medicare pays for a crisis team connected to the 988 line that comes to a person at any hour, and a screening the law makes free stays free through its lab work and the follow-up test that checks an abnormal result.

  • Medicare covers dental, eye, and hearing care, including checkups, fillings, dentures, glasses, and hearing aids, and a person under 21 pays no deductible or copay for it, including braces needed for their health.
  • A person who chooses hospice can keep treatment for the illness, a patient kept under observation pays no more than an admitted patient, and Medicare pays for daily skilled nursing care with no three-day hospital stay required first.
  • Mental illness and addiction are covered on the same terms as any other illness, with no separate limits on days, visits, or approvals.
