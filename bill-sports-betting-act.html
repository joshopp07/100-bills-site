The Sports Betting Act

The Sports Betting Act takes the easiest bets to fix off the board and protects the people who place the rest. A bet on a single moment that one player could decide alone, like a bet that a player scores under 20 points or double-faults in the next game, cannot be offered anywhere. In tennis, golf, and fighting, lower-tier events where the prize money is too small to live on lose all betting, because that is where fixing is most common. Players, coaches, and referees cannot bet on their own sport or pass inside information to anyone who does. In team sports and top-tier events, you can still bet on who wins and by how much.

  • Every betting account opens with a daily and monthly limit on what you can put in, and to go above it you have to show income or wealth at the level required to invest in private startups.
  • You can shut yourself out of every sportsbook in the country with one sign-up.
  • You cannot bet with a credit card, directly or through another account.
  • A sportsbook cannot cut your limits, give you worse odds, or close your account because you win.
  • A bonus has to say plainly what you must bet before you can cash it out, and no bonus can go to someone who has shut themselves out or reached their limit.
