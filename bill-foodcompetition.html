The Farmer Freedom and Lower Cost Groceries Act

The Farmer Freedom and Lower Cost Groceries Act gives farmers, ranchers, growers, and food suppliers a fair deal from the large companies they depend on. Farmers can fix their own equipment, or take it to any independent mechanic, with the same parts, software, and tools a dealer gets. Poultry companies cannot lower a grower’s pay rate or send fewer flocks because of a ranking against other growers, and meatpackers cannot punish a rancher for complaining to regulators, lawmakers, courts, or the press. Large grocery chains cannot rewrite a supplier’s deal on their own. Big food companies find it harder to buy competitors, and a "Product of USA" label on meat means the animal was born, raised, slaughtered, and processed here.

  • A farmer can repair a tractor, combine, or irrigation system, or use any independent mechanic, with the same parts, software, and tools the manufacturer gives its dealers, for no more than dealers pay, and the warranty still covers any defect the repair did not cause.
  • A package of meat or poultry labeled "Product of USA" has to come from an animal born, raised, slaughtered, and processed in the United States.
  • A large grocery chain cannot change a supplier’s agreed price on its own, make the supplier wait more than thirty days to be paid for perishable food, or charge the supplier for goods lost or stolen in its stores.
  • A meatpacker or poultry company cannot take away a rancher’s or grower’s contract, flocks, or pay for talking to a reporter, a legislator, or other growers, for working with other growers, or for joining a growers’ association.
  • A purchase that would leave a meatpacker, seed or fertilizer company, food manufacturer, or grocery chain with more than a set share of its market, or leave that market too concentrated, is presumed illegal.
