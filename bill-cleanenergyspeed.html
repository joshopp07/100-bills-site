The Clean Energy Speed Act

The Clean Energy Speed Act clears the way for solar on your home and clean power near you. A certified rooftop solar or battery system gets its local permit within 60 days of a complete application or a written reason why not, with fees capped in federal law, and no homeowners’ association rule can block it. Certified plug-in solar is legal in every state. Your utility cannot charge a monthly fee for solar. Solar farms and batteries on old mines and closed power plants can get federal approval without a lengthy environmental study, and a state or county cannot ban a clean energy project with its site secured and in line for the high-voltage grid.

  • A certified plug-in solar panel is legal in every state with no permit, utility agreement, fee, or meter change, and a lease can no longer ban one outright from your balcony, patio, or window.
  • Your utility cannot charge you a monthly fee, a minimum bill, or a special rate for having solar or a battery.
  • Your utility has 25 business days to approve your home solar system or name the specific engineering problem on your circuit, and if it does neither, you can turn the system on.
  • A solar farm or battery on an old mine, a well pad, or the site of a closed power plant can get its federal approval without a lengthy environmental study.
  • A state or county cannot ban a clean energy project that has secured its site and is in line to connect to the high-voltage grid, and your town keeps its building and safety codes.
