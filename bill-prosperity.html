The People's Prosperity Act

The People’s Prosperity Act gives every American, and every worker authorized to work here, an investment account that lasts from birth through retirement and follows you from job to job, with nothing to apply for. A child born a citizen starts life with money from the government already in the account. The money is yours, held in plain index funds at a capped fee. The bill builds on the savings account the right proposed and the child version it enacted in 2025, adding automatic enrollment and a government match, and making the starting deposit permanent for every child born a citizen.

  • If you work for an employer, a small share of your pay goes into your account automatically and rises each year up to a set maximum, and you can stop at any time in a single step.
  • At the lowest incomes the government pays all or part of that share for a citizen, and it matches what a citizen puts in on the first $7,500 a year, at the highest rate for those who earn least.
  • Once your balance reaches a set minimum, you can borrow up to half of it with no credit check and no fixed payment schedule, and the interest you pay goes back into your own account. In an emergency such as a medical bill, an eviction notice, a car you need for work breaking down, or a lost job, an adult can withdraw without penalty and without paperwork up front.
  • Your account does not count against you for SNAP, SSI, federally assisted housing, federal student aid, or Medicaid’s doctor and hospital coverage, and debt collectors cannot seize it.
  • Each year the Bureau of Labor Statistics certifies the Middle-Class Growth Rate: how fast pay grows for workers in the middle of the wage scale, and how many of them have full-time work. The raises of the President, the Vice President, the Cabinet, and Congress are tied to it under the [Executive Integrity Act](law.html?bill=executive) and the [Congressional Integrity Act](law.html?bill=congressional), so those who govern get a raise only when the middle class does. The statistics behind it are published for anyone tracking how working Americans are doing.
