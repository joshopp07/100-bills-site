The Judiciary Integrity Act

The Judiciary Integrity Act holds federal judges, including the Justices of the Supreme Court, to a written ethics code and lets the public check on it. A private flight or a resort stay has to be reported at its full value. Each judge’s reported investments, and the family’s, are checked against the companies in every case, and the judge is told of any match. Each judge is offered a stricter rule limiting what they may own, and you can look up who accepted it. A separate part sets eighteen-year terms for federal judges and adds judgeships.

  • Anyone can read the records and dockets of every federal court online without paying a fee.
  • A company or group with a case before a judge, or that appeared before that judge in the past three years, cannot give the judge a private flight or a resort stay.
  • Every Supreme Court order deciding a petition or an emergency request, even one issued without argument, shows how each Justice voted and who did not take part.
  • You can see who paid for an outside group’s written argument in a federal court and who put large sums into ads for or against a nominee to the federal bench.
  • Law clerks and other court employees can report misconduct to an independent office outside their court, cannot be punished for reporting, and are not bound by any agreement that would keep them quiet.
