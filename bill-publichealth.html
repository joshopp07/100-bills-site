The Public Health Guarantee Act

In 2025, the federal government moved to cancel about $11 billion in public health grants to states, and a federal judge blocked the cut for the states that sued, while the case continues. This bill gives the health department that protects your community steady federal money every year, rising over five years to at least $4.5 billion a year nationwide, so it can track and investigate disease, prevent chronic illness and injury, guard against environmental hazards, and respond to emergencies. Test results for reportable diseases reach health officials directly, and that information cannot be used against you for immigration or policing, used to decide your coverage, or sold.

  • When a community asks for help, licensed health workers from other states can come without first obtaining a new license.
  • Funding for global disease tracking and U.S. dues to the international health organizations it belongs to are permanent, and no official can withhold that money on a ground the law does not state.
  • Members of the expert committee behind your vaccine recommendations serve fixed six-year terms and can be removed only for neglect, malfeasance, or a disqualifying conflict, and their recommendations stay in force even if seats sit empty.
  • EPA repealed its finding that greenhouse gas pollution endangers your health in 2026. This bill writes that finding into law, cancels the repeal, and bars any agency from reversing it.
  • High-containment laboratories that handle dangerous germs are inspected every year by independent inspectors, and research that could make a pandemic germ spread faster or kill more people needs independent review first, whoever pays for it.
