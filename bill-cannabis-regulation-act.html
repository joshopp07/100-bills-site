The Cannabis Regulation Act

The Cannabis Regulation Act takes marijuana off the federal list of controlled substances and leaves each State to decide whether to allow it. Cannabis is taxed like alcohol and kept from kids like tobacco, with no sales to anyone under 21 and no flavored vapes. People serving federal marijuana sentences are released unless their sentencing record shows a leadership role or a weapon. Most old federal marijuana convictions clear automatically, without anyone filing. In Michigan, where people must apply to clear a record, only 6.5 percent did so within five years.

  • Marijuana comes off the federal list of controlled substances, and each State decides whether to allow it within its borders.
  • People serving federal marijuana sentences are released within 60 days unless their sentencing record shows a leadership role or a weapon.
  • Most old federal marijuana convictions clear automatically, without anyone filing.
  • No one under 21 can buy cannabis, and no inhaled cannabis product can carry a flavor other than cannabis.
