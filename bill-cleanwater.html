The Safe Water and Air Act

No family should be poisoned by its tap water or air. The Safe Water and Air Act gets the lead pipes in every water system’s inventory out of the ground within ten years, the whole line, at no charge to the people who live with them, and with a free filter during the work. Newark replaced more than 23,000 lead lines in under three years. Limits on two forever chemicals in tap water are set in law, and the executive branch can make the standards this bill sets stricter but never looser.

  • The limits on two forever chemicals in your tap water, PFOA and PFOS, stay at the 2024 levels, and the other forever chemicals in the 2024 rule get limits at least as strict by a set date.
  • If you rent, your landlord cannot pass the cost of replacing your lead line on to you, in rent or any other charge.
  • If you live near a refinery or another plant the bill covers, you can see the pollution readings at its fence line as they are taken, free of charge, and when they pass a set level the plant has to find the cause and fix it.
  • A neighborhood with heavy measured pollution cannot get a new major polluting plant, or a major expansion of one, if the total passes set limits, unless it adds no net pollution.
  • Farmers, builders, and families know which streams and wetlands federal law protects, because Congress writes the definition into the law, and no administration can narrow it.
